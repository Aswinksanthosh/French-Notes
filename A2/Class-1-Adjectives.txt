French Adjectives Lesson - A2 Class 1

CORE GRAMMAR COVERED

An adjective describes or provides information about a noun, including qualities, colors, size, form, and opinions.

Adjectives agree with the noun in gender and number:
- Masculine/feminine agreement with forms such as: grand/grande, petit/petite, lent/lente, actif/active
- Plural adjectives generally take -s; feminine plural generally takes -e then -s
- For mixed-gender groups, masculine plural is used

ADJECTIVE FORMATION PATTERNS

When masculine adjective ends with consonant: add E for feminine
Examples: grand → grande, petit → petite

When adjective already ends with -e: remains the same in feminine
Examples: calme (both masculine and feminine)

Formation patterns for gender agreement:
- -f → -ve: sportif/sportive, actif/active
- -x → -se: heureux/heureuse, dangereux/dangereuse
- -er → -ère: cher/chère, léger/légère
- -eux → -euse: curieux/curieuse, courageux/courageuse
- -if → -ive: créatif/créative, attentif/attentive
- -ien → -ienne: ancien/ancienne, européen/européenne, musicien/musicienne
- -on → -onne: bon/bonne, mignon/mignonne
- -et → -ette: (pattern example)

Irregular forms (learn individually):
- beau/bel (handsome/beautiful)
- nouveau/nouvel (new)
- vieux/vieil (old)
- blanc/blanche (white)
- frais/fraîche (fresh)
- doux/douce (soft/sweet)

PLURAL AGREEMENT

Generally add S: grand/grands, grande/grandes

Adjectives ending in -s or -x generally do not change in plural
Examples: heureux/heureux, heureuse/heureuses

Some adjectives ending in -al change to -aux:
- national/nationaux
- original/originaux
- général/généraux

ADJECTIVE POSITION AND MEANING

Most qualifying adjectives follow the noun.
Examples: une voiture rouge (red car), un étudiant français (French student), une table ronde (round table)

Frequently used adjectives for BEAUTY, AGE, GOODNESS, and SIZE often precede the noun:
- un petit garçon (small boy)
- une grande maison (big house)
- un beau film (beautiful film)
- une jeune femme (young woman)
- bon (good), mauvais (bad), meilleur (best)

Colors, nationality, shape, material, and many descriptive adjectives follow the noun:
- une voiture rouge (red car)
- un étudiant français (French student)
- une chemise blanche (white shirt)
- un problème difficile (difficult problem)
- une histoire intéressante (interesting story)

IMPORTANT: Adjective position can change meaning
- ancien professeur (former teacher) vs un professeur ancien (an old teacher)
- un grand homme (a great man) vs un homme grand (a tall man)
- un pauvre homme (an unfortunate man) vs un homme pauvre (a poor man financially)

COLOR ADJECTIVES

Colors agree with the noun when they're adjectives:
- un sac vert (a green bag)
- une voiture verte (a green car)
- des sacs verts (green bags)

Invariable colors (don't change):
- orange: des robes orange (orange dresses)
- marron (brown): des pantalons marrons (brown pants)

EPITHET vs PREDICATE ADJECTIVES

Adjectif épithète: directly accompanies the noun (before or after)
Examples: une voiture rapide (fast car), un joli jardin (pretty garden), un vélo blanc (white bicycle)

Adjectif attribut: separated from noun by a verb, still agrees with subject
Examples: la voiture est rapide (the car is fast), le jardin est joli (the garden is pretty)

Verbs that separate adjectives:
- être (to be)
- sembler (to seem)
- paraître (to appear)
- devenir (to become)

SPECIAL FORMS

Three frequently used adjectives have special masculine forms before nouns starting with vowel or silent h:

1. beau → bel: un bel homme (a handsome man), un bel oiseau (a beautiful bird)
2. nouveau → nouvel: une nouvelle abonnement (a new subscription)
3. vieux → vieil: un vieil homme (an old man)

Use these three forms when adjective precedes a masculine noun beginning with a vowel or silent h.

ADJECTIVES USED AS NOUNS

Adjectives can sometimes function as nouns when used with an article:
- les riches (the rich)
- les pauvres (the poor)
- un jeune (a young person)
Example: J'ai vu un jeune hier traverser la route (I saw a young person yesterday crossing the road)

- un bon moment (a good time)

Expression to learn:
- avoir l'air + adjective = to look/appear
Example: Ils ont l'air heureux (They look happy)
