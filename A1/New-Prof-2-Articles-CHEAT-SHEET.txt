⚡ THE RULE: Always learn nouns WITH their articles!
   "la maison" not just "maison"
   "le livre" not just "livre"

================================================================================
📍 DEFINITE ARTICLES (THE = "specific/known things")
================================================================================

LE = masculine singular
  • le garçon (the boy)
  • le livre (the book)
  • le café (the café)

LA = feminine singular
  • la fille (the girl)
  • la maison (the house)
  • la solution (the solution)

LES = plural (ANY gender)
  • les enfants (the children)
  • les livres (the books)
  • les maisons (the houses)

L' = before vowels/silent h (le → l', la → l')
  • l'école (the school)
  • l'ami (the friend)
  • l'appareil (the device)
  • l'hôpital (the hospital)

================================================================================
📍 INDEFINITE ARTICLES (A/SOME = "unspecific/unknown things")
================================================================================

UN = masculine singular
  • un garçon (a boy)
  • un livre (a book)
  • un ami (a friend)

UNE = feminine singular
  • une fille (a girl)
  • une maison (a house)
  • une école (a school)

DES = plural (ANY gender, means "some")
  • des enfants (children/some children)
  • des livres (books/some books)
  • des maisons (houses/some houses)

================================================================================
🎯 QUICK COMPARISON TABLE
================================================================================

Type          | Masculine Sg. | Feminine Sg. | Plural     | Before Vowels
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEFINITE      | LE            | LA           | LES        | L'
INDEFINITE    | UN            | UNE          | DES        | (use UN/UNE)
ENGLISH       | THE           | THE          | THE        | THE
MEANING       | Specific      | Specific     | Specific   | Specific

================================================================================
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================

❌ WRONG → ✅ CORRECT (REASON)
1. "le école" → "l'école" (vowel rule)
2. "un maison" → "une maison" (feminine noun)
3. "la ami" → "l'ami" (vowel rule)
4. "les enfant" → "les enfants" (plural needs -s)
5. "de les" → "des" (contraction rule)
6. "à le" → "au" (contraction rule)

================================================================================
💡 USAGE TIPS
================================================================================

✓ USE DEFINITE (le/la/les) WHEN:
  • Talking about something specific you know
  • Referring to something mentioned before
  • Making general statements about things
  Example: "Je vois LE garçon" (I see the specific boy)

✓ USE INDEFINITE (un/une/des) WHEN:
  • Talking about something for the first time
  • It's not specific or you don't know which one
  • Making general statements
  Example: "Je vois UN garçon" (I see a/some boy)

🎧 PRONUNCIATION REMINDERS
================================================================================

LE = "luh" (neutral vowel)
LA = "lah" (short a sound)
LES = "lay" (like "lay down")
L' = "l" (just the L sound)

UN = "uhn" (nasal, one sound)
UNE = "oon" (nasal, one sound)
DES = "day" (like "day")

================================================================================
🔗 ARTICLE CONTRACTION RULES
================================================================================

These are NOT new articles - just how articles combine with prepositions:

à + le = AU (to/at the)
à + les = AUX (to/at the - plural)

de + le = DU (from/of the)
de + les = DES (from/of the - plural)

Examples:
- Je vais AU parc (I go to the park)
- Il parle AUX enfants (He talks to the children)
- Je reviens DU café (I come from the café)
- C'est à nous DES livres (These books belong to us)
