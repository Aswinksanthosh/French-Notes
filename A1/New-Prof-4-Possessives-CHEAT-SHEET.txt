⚡ CRITICAL RULE: Possessives agree with NOUN GENDER, NOT SPEAKER GENDER!

If noun is masculine → use masculine possessive
If noun is feminine → use feminine possessive
(It doesn't matter if YOU are male or female!)

================================================================================
SINGULAR POSSESSORS (1 person owns something)
================================================================================

MY (mon/ma/mon):
  • Masc: mon livre, mon ami → "mon" (my book/friend)
  • Fem (consonant): ma maison, ma sœur → "ma" (my house/sister)
  • Fem (vowel): mon école, mon amie → "mon" (my school/friend - for pronunciation!)

YOUR (ton/ta/ton):
  • Masc: ton livre, ton ami → "ton"
  • Fem (consonant): ta maison, ta sœur → "ta"
  • Fem (vowel): ton école, ton amie → "ton"

HIS/HER (son/sa/son):
  • Masc: son livre, son ami → "son"
  • Fem (consonant): sa maison, sa sœur → "sa"
  • Fem (vowel): son école, son amie → "son"

================================================================================
PLURAL POSSESSORS (2+ people own something)
================================================================================

OUR (notre/nos):
  • Sing noun: notre maison, notre ami → "notre" (our house/friend)
  • Plural noun: nos maisons, nos amis → "nos" (our houses/friends)

YOUR - Formal/Plural (votre/vos):
  • Sing noun: votre livre, votre maison → "votre"
  • Plural noun: vos livres, vos maisons → "vos"

THEIR (leur/leurs):
  • Sing noun: leur ami, leur maison → "leur"
  • Plural noun: leurs amis, leurs maisons → "leurs"

================================================================================
THE PRONUNCIATION TRICK: MON/TON/SON before VOWEL WORDS
================================================================================

French doesn't like vowel sounds colliding!

❌ ma école (sounds terrible - ma + école)
✅ mon école (sounds smooth - mon + école)

❌ ta amie (awkward)
✅ ton amie (natural!)

❌ sa armoire (ugly sound)
✅ son armoire (better!)

This applies to FEMININE nouns starting with vowels/silent h only!

================================================================================
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================

Wrong           | Correct       | Reason
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
ma école        | mon école     | Feminine + vowel: use mon
sa ami          | son ami       | Feminine + vowel: use son
ta armoire      | ton armoire   | Feminine + vowel: use ton
mes maison      | ma maison     | Singular: use ma, not mes
tes amis        | tes amis      | OK - plural!
notre livre     | notre livre   | OK - singular!
nos maisons     | nos maisons   | OK - plural!
mon crayon      | ma crayon     | "crayon" is masculine: use mon!

================================================================================
🎯 GENDER AGREEMENT: The Core Issue
================================================================================

Remember: The possessive DEPENDS ON THE NOUN, not the person speaking!

Example: Even if YOU are a woman, if you own a masculine book:
  → mon livre (my book) NOT "ma livre"

Even if YOU are a man, if you own a feminine house:
  → ma maison (my house) NOT "mon maison"

The NOUN'S gender drives the choice!
