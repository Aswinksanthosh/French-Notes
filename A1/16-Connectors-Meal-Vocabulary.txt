### Grammar: pour + infinitive

- Whenever pour ("for"/"in order to") is followed by a verb, that verb is automatically in the infinitive form (the un-conjugated, dictionary form).
- J'aime aller à la maison pour manger avant de retourner à l'école.
- — I like to go home to eat before going back to school.
- Note: French "pour + infinitive" often translates to English "to + verb" (not "for + -ing").

### Grammar: Connectors — parce que vs à cause de

| Connector | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| parce que | because — followed by a full clause (subject + verb) | L'Espagne a gagné parce qu'ils ont bien joué. (Spain won because they played well.) |
| à cause de | because of — followed by a noun or pronoun only | Je ne suis pas venu à cause de toi. (I didn't come because of you.) |

- Rule of thumb: parce que = subject + verb (+ noun) follows. à cause de = only a noun/pronoun follows, no verb. They cannot be swapped.

### Grammar: mais (opposition) / sans (without) / avec (with)

| Connector | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| mais | but (opposition) | J'ai une voiture mais je prends le bus. (I have a car but I take the bus.) |
| sans | without | On préfère le centre ville sans voiture. (We prefer downtown without a car.) |
| avec | with | Avec ma femme, on prend le bus. (With my wife, we take the bus.) |

### Grammar: devoir — verb vs noun

- Devoir as a verb = "to have to." Un devoir as a noun = "a task/homework/assignment."
- Qu'est-ce que vous devez apporter? — What do you have to bring? (verb)
- C'est un devoir. — It's a homework/task. (noun)

### Compound words (mots composés)

- Some word pairs combine into one meaning and shouldn't be translated word-for-word.
- grands-parents = grandparents (not "big parents")
- petit-déjeuner = breakfast (not "small lunch")

### Listening Practice: Voicemail (Dinner Invitation)

Allô, c'est Solange, je t'appelle pour confirmer le dîner samedi soir à la maison, vous pouvez venir vers 20h00. Est-ce que vous pouvez apporter 2 baguettes? Nous avons aussi invité Didier et Michel, ils apportent le dessert, une tarte aux pommes. Bon alors à samedi.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who is calling? | Solange |
| What day is the dinner? | Samedi soir (Saturday evening) |
| What time? | 20h00 (8:00 PM) |
| What do they need to bring? | 2 baguettes |
| Who else was invited? | Didier and Michel (bringing dessert — apple pie) |

### Time expressions: reading "00" minutes

- When minutes are "00," there are a few ways to say it:
- midi = noon (12h00)  |  minuit = midnight (00h00)
- 20h00 can be said as "vingt heures" or "vingt heures pile" (8:00 PM sharp)
