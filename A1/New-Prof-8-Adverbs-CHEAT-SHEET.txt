✨ ADVERBS: Words that modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs
   (Usually end in -ment, modify HOW/WHEN/WHERE something happens)

================================================================================
FORMING ADVERBS: THE -MENT SUFFIX RULE
================================================================================

BASIC RULE: Take adjective + add -ment

Adjective Ending in Consonant → Add -ment:
  lent (slow) → lentement (slowly)
  heureux (happy) → heureusement (happily)
  facile (easy) → facilement (easily)
  intelligent (intelligent) → intelligemment (intelligently)

Adjective Ending in E → Drop E, add -ment:
  seul (sole/alone) → seulement (only)
  vrai (true) → vraiment (truly/really)
  absolu (absolute) → absolument (absolutely)
  certain (certain) → certainement (certainly)

Adjective Ending in -ant → Change to -amment:
  constant (constant) → constamment (constantly)
  courant (current) → couramment (fluently)
  différent (different) → différemment (differently)

Adjective Ending in -ent → Change to -emment:
  différent (different) → différemment (differently)
  prudent (prudent) → prudemment (prudently)
  patient (patient) → patiemment (patiently)

================================================================================
ADVERB POSITION IN SENTENCE
================================================================================

⚠️ GOLDEN RULE: Adverbs usually come AFTER the verb

With simple tense (present, imparfait):
  Je parle lentement (I speak slowly)
  Elle travaille sérieusement (She works seriously)
  Nous mangeons rapidement (We eat quickly)

With passé composé (2-word past):
  Adverbs go AFTER the past participle usually:
  Je suis allé rapidement (I went quickly)
  Elle a parlé sérieusement (She spoke seriously)
  Ils ont travaillé diligent... (They worked diligently)

Common adverbs (bien, mal, vite, tôt, tard) go BEFORE past participle:
  Je n'ai pas bien compris (I didn't understand well)
  Elle a mal dormi (She slept badly)

================================================================================
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================

Wrong                      | Correct                | Rule
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
rapidement → rapide       | rapidement             | Adverb modifies verb
Il parle très rapidement  | Il parle très rapidement| Word order OK
sérieusement aller        | aller sérieusement     | Adverb after verb
bien → goodement          | bien                   | bien is irregular
mieux → better-ment       | mieux                  | mieux is irregular
lent                      | lentement              | Need -ment suffix
heureusly                 | heureusement           | -ment spelling
j'ai rapide parlé         | j'ai parlé rapidement  | After past participle
