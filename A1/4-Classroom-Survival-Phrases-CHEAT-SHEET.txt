CLASS 4: CLASSROOM SURVIVAL PHRASES — QUICK REFERENCE SHEET
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📝 ESSENTIAL PHRASES (Learn these first!)
─────────────────────────────────────────

1. ASKING FOR REPETITION (When you don't hear/understand)
   French: Vous pouvez répéter, s'il vous plaît ?
   English: Can you repeat, please?
   When to use: When confused or didn't catch what teacher said
   Pronoun: VOUS (formal) — always with teacher

2. ASKING FOR SPELLING (When you want to know how to spell)
   French: Comment ça s'écrit ?
   English: How do you spell it?
   When to use: When you see a word and want to know its spelling
   Pronunciation help: Comment (how) + ça (it) + s'écrit (spells itself)

3. SAYING "I DON'T UNDERSTAND"
   French: Je ne comprends pas.
   English: I don't understand.
   When to use: When lesson/instruction is confusing
   Key: "NE" must come BEFORE the verb comprendre

4. ASKING HOW TO SAY SOMETHING IN FRENCH
   French: Comment on dit [English word] en français ?
   English: How do we say [English word] in French?
   When to use: When you want to know the French word for something
   Example: Comment on dit "pen" en français? → Un stylo
   Key: Use "ON" (we/one), not just "dit"

5. APOLOGIZING FOR BEING LATE
   French: Excusez-moi, je suis en retard.
   English: Excuse me, I'm late.
   When to use: When arriving late to class
   Fixed phrase: "EN RETARD" (in late) — not just "tard"

6. POLITENESS WORDS
   French: s'il vous plaît (formal)
   English: please (with teachers/adults)

   French: s'il te plaît (informal)
   English: please (with friends/classmates)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISTAKE #1: Forgetting "NE" in negation
❌ Je comprends pas.
✓ Je ne comprends pas.
WHY: French negation needs TWO parts: ne... pas

MISTAKE #2: Mixing up TU and VOUS politeness
❌ Vous pouvez répéter, s'il te plaît?
✓ Vous pouvez répéter, s'il vous plaît?
WHY: When using VOUS, always use "vous plaît" not "te plaît"

MISTAKE #3: Wrong French for "late"
❌ Je suis tard.
✓ Je suis en retard.
WHY: "En retard" is the fixed phrase in French

MISTAKE #4: Wrong word order for "how do you say"
❌ Ça dit comment en français?
✓ Comment on dit... en français?
WHY: Question word (comment) goes FIRST in French

MISTAKE #5: Wrong verb form for "spell"
❌ Ça écrit comment?
✓ Comment ça s'écrit?
WHY: Use "s'écrit" (it spells itself), not just "écrit"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🎯 TU vs VOUS RULE (MOST IMPORTANT!)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TU = Informal "you"
   → Use with: friends, family, classmates your age
   → s'il te plaît (please — informal)
   → Tu peux répéter? (Can you repeat? — to a friend)

VOUS = Formal "you"
   → Use with: teachers, adults, strangers, people you don't know
   → s'il vous plaît (please — formal)
   → Vous pouvez répéter? (Can you repeat? — to a teacher)

IN THE CLASSROOM: Always use VOUS with your teacher!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
📚 PRONUNCIATION TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Comment ça s'écrit?     → kom-ON sa say-KREE
(C like in "cough" + ON blend)

Vous pouvez répéter?    → voo poo-vay ray-pay-TAY
(Final T is pronounced)

s'il vous plaît         → see-loo-voo-PLEH
(Ends with soft "eh" sound, not "et")

Je ne comprends pas     → zhuh nuh kom-PROHN pah
(NE blends with je = "nuh", comprends is com-PREN)

Excusez-moi            → EHX-kew-zay-MWAH
(Final Z = silent S sound before vowel)

🎓 KEY GRAMMAR INSIGHT (From Professor's Notes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMPORTANT: "..." (ellipsis) in "Comment on dit ... en français?"
→ The three dots are a PLACEHOLDER for whatever word you want to ask about
→ Replace them with the actual word: "Comment on dit PIZZA en français?"
→ This is how you actively build vocabulary in real class!
