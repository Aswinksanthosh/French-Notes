                    FRENCH NOUN GENDER - QUICK REFERENCE

⏱️ MEMORIZE THIS FIRST: Learn nouns WITH their articles!
   "la nature" → not just "nature"
   "le village" → not just "village"

================================================================================
🎯 MASCULINE PATTERNS (LE/UN)
================================================================================

1. Nouns ending in -MENT = MASCULINE
   gouvernement, département, appartement, jugement, paiement

2. Nouns ending in -AGE = MASCULINE
   village, fromage, voyage, garage, passage, courage, bagage

3. Nouns ending in -OIR = MASCULINE
   miroir, tiroir, escalier (wait—this is -ier, also masculine!)

4. Nouns ending in -ISME = MASCULINE
   tourisme, nationalisme, communisme, optimisme

5. ENGLISH LOAN WORDS = MASCULINE
   le week-end, le parking, le shopping, le email, le business
   (Default: masculine when borrowed from English)

6. DAYS, MONTHS, SEASONS = ALL MASCULINE
   Days: lundi, mardi, mercredi, jeudi, vendredi, samedi, dimanche
   Months: janvier, février, mars, avril, mai, juin, juillet, août, septembre, octobre, novembre, décembre
   Seasons: le printemps, l'été, l'automne, l'hiver

================================================================================
🎯 FEMININE PATTERNS (LA/UNE)
================================================================================

1. Nouns ending in -TION or -SION = FEMININE
   connexion, option, décision, nation, profession, session

2. Nouns ending in -URE = FEMININE
   nature, culture, future, lecture, signature, literature

3. Nouns ending in -ADE = FEMININE
   promenade, salade, escapade, grenade, cascade

4. Nouns ending in -E = USUALLY FEMININE (BUT CHECK EXCEPTIONS!)
   table, porte, chaise, maison, personne, histoire

   ⚠️ EXCEPTIONS (Masculine despite -E):
   - père (father)
   - frère (brother)
   - monde (world)
   - musée (museum)

================================================================================
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================

❌ WRONG → ✅ CORRECT (REASON)
1. un nature → la nature (-ure = feminine)
2. la village → le village (-age = masculine)
3. le solution → la solution (-tion = feminine)
4. la gouvernement → le gouvernement (-ment = masculine)

================================================================================
💡 STUDY TIPS
================================================================================

✓ TIP 1: Memorize articles WITH words, not separately
  ✓ Say "la table" not "table"
  ✓ Say "le village" not "village"

✓ TIP 2: Create flashcards with ARTICLE INCLUDED
  Front: [image/context]
  Back: LE village or LA nature (with full article)

✓ TIP 3: Group by pattern - easier to remember!
  -MENT words: gouvernement, jugement, paiement
  -AGE words: village, fromage, voyage
  -TION words: décision, option, connexion

✓ TIP 4: When you learn a NEW noun, learn gender FIRST
  New word? → Find out LE or LA immediately
  Don't separate article from word!

✓ TIP 5: Make a personal "exception list"
  - père (father) - MASCULINE
  - frère (brother) - MASCULINE
  - Other exceptions YOU struggle with
