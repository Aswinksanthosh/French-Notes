PART 1: SUBJECT PRONOUNS (LES PRONOMS SUJETS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY PRONOUNS MATTER:
In French, the pronoun you use tells a LOT:
  • TU = intimate/casual (friends, family, kids)
  • VOUS = formal/respectful (strangers, bosses, older people)

❌ Using TU with your boss = RUDE
✅ Using VOUS with strangers = POLITE

---

THE PRONOUNS:

Pronoun | Meaning              | When Used              | Example
--------|----------------------|------------------------|------------------
JE      | I                    | Always for yourself    | Je suis étudiant(e)
TU      | You (singular)       | Friends, family, kids  | Tu es français?
IL      | He                   | For males/masculine    | Il est là
ELLE    | She                  | For females/feminine   | Elle est ici
NOUS    | We                   | For a group with you   | Nous sommes amis
VOUS    | You (formal/plural)  | Strangers, plural you  | Vous allez bien?
ILS     | They (mixed group)   | Any group with males   | Ils sont là
ELLES   | They (all females)   | Only all-female groups | Elles sont là

---

IMPORTANT RULES:

🔴 RULE #1: TU vs VOUS (The Golden Rule of French!)

TU = Informal (use with):
  • Friends
  • Family members
  • Children
  • People your age in casual settings

  Example conversation:
  Friend: Ça va?          ("How's it going?")
  You: Ça va bien!        ("I'm doing well!")

VOUS = Formal/Plural (use with):
  • Strangers or people you just met
  • Bosses or authority figures
  • Elderly people (respectful)
  • Multiple people (regardless of relationship)

  Example conversation:
  Stranger: Comment allez-vous?   ("How are you?" - formal)
  You: Je vais bien, merci.       ("I'm doing well, thank you")

💡 GOLDEN RULE: When in doubt, use VOUS. It's never rude. Using TU when you shouldn't is VERY rude.

---

🔴 RULE #2: ILS vs ELLES (Gender Rule)

ILS = "They" (masculine plural or MIXED GROUP)
ELLES = "They" (feminine plural - ALL FEMALES ONLY)

Example:
  • 5 women = ELLES sont là         ("They are here")
  • 5 men = ILS sont là              ("They are here")
  • 4 women + 1 man = ILS sont là    ("They are here" - ONE man makes it masculine!)

💡 TRICKY: English "they" has no gender, but French does! One male in the group = use ILS

---

🔴 RULE #3: J'AI NOT "JE AI"

When a pronoun ends in a vowel (JE, TE, LE) and the next word starts with a vowel,
we DROP the vowel and use an apostrophe. This is called ELISION.

Examples:
  • JE + AVOIR = J'AI (not "je ai")
  • LE + APPLE = L'APPLE (not "le apple")
  • TE + AIMER = T'AIMER (not "te aimer")

Why? French doesn't like two vowel sounds in a row!

PART 2: TIMES OF DAY (LES MOMENTS DE LA JOURNÉE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Understanding times of day is important because your GREETINGS CHANGE!
You don't say "Bonjour" at midnight - you say "Bonne nuit"!

Time of Day | French         | When Used              | Greeting to Use
------------|----------------|------------------------|------------------
Morning     | le matin       | 6am - 11:30am         | Bonjour
Noon        | midi           | 12:00pm (exactly)     | Bonjour
Afternoon   | l'après-midi   | 12:30pm - 5:30pm      | Bon après-midi
Evening     | le soir        | 5:30pm - 10:00pm      | Bonsoir
Night       | la nuit        | 10:00pm - 6:00am      | Bonne nuit
Midnight    | minuit         | 12:00am (exactly)     | Bonne nuit

---

EXAMPLES:
  • 7:00 AM = "Bonjour! Comment allez-vous?" (Good morning!)
  • 2:00 PM = "Bon après-midi!" (Good afternoon!)
  • 7:00 PM = "Bonsoir!" (Good evening!)
  • 11:30 PM = "Bonne nuit!" (Good night!)

PART 3: GREETINGS & FAREWELLS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FORMAL GREETINGS (with VOUS - use with strangers, older people, professionals):

Greeting              | Pronunciation         | When to Use
--------------------|------------------------|----------------------------------
Bonjour madame!      | bon-zhoor muh-dam     | Good morning/hello, ma'am
Bonjour mademoiselle!| bon-zhoor mad-mwah-zel| Good morning/hello, young lady
Bonjour monsieur!    | bon-zhoor muh-syuh    | Good morning/hello, sir
Comment allez-vous?  | ko-mahnt ah-lay-voo   | How are you? (formal)
Vous allez bien?     | voo ah-lay bee-in     | Are you doing well?
Et vous?             | ay voo                | And you?

---

--------------------|----------------------|------------------

Comment vas-tu?     | ko-mahnt vah too     | How are you? (casual)

---

SAYING GOODBYE:

Goodbye Phrase      | Pronunciation         | When to Use

---

EXAMPLE CONVERSATIONS:

FORMAL SCENARIO (Meeting someone for the first time):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Stranger: Bonjour monsieur. Comment allez-vous?
You:      Bonjour. Je vais bien, merci. Et vous?
Stranger: Très bien, merci!
You:      Au revoir!

INFORMAL SCENARIO (Meeting a friend):
──────────────────────────────────────
Friend: Salut! Ça va?
You:    Ça va bien! Et toi?
Friend: Super! À plus tard!
You:    À bientôt!

PART 4: ÊTRE (TO BE) - THE MOST IMPORTANT VERB
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ÊTRE = TO BE
This is THE most important verb in French. You use it CONSTANTLY.

Conjugation (Present Tense):

I am               | je suis           | zhuh swee
You are (informal) | tu es             | too eh
He is              | il est            | eel eh
She is             | elle est          | el eh
We are             | nous sommes       | noo som
You are (formal)   | vous êtes         | voo zet
They are           | ils/elles sont    | eel/el son

---

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

Personal:
  • Je suis français(e) = I am French
  • Tu es américain(e) = You are American
  • Elle est étudiante = She is a student
  • Nous sommes amis = We are friends

Describing feelings:
  • Je suis heureux = I am happy
  • Tu es triste = You are sad
  • Vous êtes fatigué = You are tired

Locations:
  • Je suis ici = I am here
  • Il est là = He is there
  • Nous sommes à Paris = We are in Paris

---

💡 KEY POINT: ÊTRE is IRREGULAR
The forms don't follow a pattern! You must memorize:
  je suis, tu es, il/elle est, nous sommes, vous êtes, ils/elles sont

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 5: AVOIR (TO HAVE) - EQUALLY IMPORTANT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AVOIR = TO HAVE
This is the second most important verb! Used for possession and in compound tenses.

Conjugation (Present Tense):

I have             | j'ai              | zhay
You have (informal)| tu as             | too ah
He has             | il a              | eel ah
She has            | elle a            | el ah
We have            | nous avons        | noo zah-von
You have (formal)  | vous avez         | voo zah-vay
They have          | ils/elles ont     | eel/el on

---

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

Possession:
  • J'ai un chat = I have a cat
  • Tu as un chien = You have a dog
  • Il a une maison = He has a house
  • Nous avons des amis = We have friends

Age (French uses AVOIR for age, not ÊTRE!):
  • J'ai 25 ans = I am 25 years old (literally: "I have 25 years")
  • Tu as quel âge? = How old are you?
  • Elle a 30 ans = She is 30 years old

---

💡 KEY POINT: AVOIR is also IRREGULAR
The forms don't follow a pattern! You must memorize:
  j'ai, tu as, il/elle a, nous avons, vous avez, ils/elles ont

---

⚠️ IMPORTANT: HOW TO SAY AGE IN FRENCH

In English: "I am 25 years old"
In French: "J'ai 25 ans" (literally: "I have 25 years")

This confuses English speakers! French uses AVOIR (have), not ÊTRE (be), for age.

Example:
  Incorrect: "Je suis 25 ans"  ❌
  Correct: "J'ai 25 ans"       ✅

PART 7: COMMON MISTAKES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ MISTAKE #1: Using TU with someone you shouldn't
❌ WRONG: "Tu vas bien, monsieur?" (to your boss)
✅ RIGHT: "Vous allez bien, monsieur?"

---

❌ MISTAKE #2: Forgetting the ELISION with J'AI
❌ WRONG: "Je ai un chat"
✅ RIGHT: "J'ai un chat"

---

❌ MISTAKE #3: Using ÊTRE for age instead of AVOIR
❌ WRONG: "Je suis 25 ans"
✅ RIGHT: "J'ai 25 ans"

---

❌ MISTAKE #4: Using ELLES for a mixed group
❌ WRONG: "Ils et elles sont là = ELLES sont là"
✅ RIGHT: "Ils et elles sont là = ILS sont là"

---

❌ MISTAKE #5: Irregular verb forms
❌ WRONG: "Je suis suis" (trying to regularize être)
✅ RIGHT: Just memorize - there's no pattern!
