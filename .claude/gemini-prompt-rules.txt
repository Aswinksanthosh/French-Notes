HOW TO COPY FULL CHAPTER HTML (RECOMMENDED FOR BEST RESULTS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY COPY HTML?
When you copy the full HTML of your lesson (exactly as it appears on screen), Gemini can see:
✓ All content and structure exactly as you see it
✓ Tables, lists, formatted sections, examples
✓ The visual organization and emphasis
✓ Expandable sections and all details
✓ Common mistakes and real-world examples

HOW TO COPY THE CHAPTER:
1. Open your French Notes website (index.html)
2. Find the chapter you want to study
3. Right-click on the chapter section → "Inspect" (or press F12)
4. In the Inspector, find the <div> that contains your chapter
   - Look for: <div class="lesson-card" id="classXX">
   - This div contains the ENTIRE chapter
5. Right-click on this <div> → "Copy" → "Copy element"
6. Paste into your Gemini prompt where it says "[COPY THE FULL HTML...]"

EXAMPLE:
The HTML will look something like:
<div class="lesson-card" id="class21">
  <div class="lesson-header">
    <h2>A2: CLASS 1 — French Adjectives</h2>
  </div>

  <button class="copy-btn">💡 Copy Gemini Prompt</button>

  <p class="topic">Topic: Adjective agreement, placement, formation patterns...</p>

  <h3>📚 Learning Objectives</h3>
  <div class="note">
  Understand and apply adjective agreement...
  </div>

  <h3>Grammar: Adjective Agreement</h3>
  <div class="expandable-section">
    <button class="expand-btn">🎧 Adjective Agreement Explained</button>
    ...
  </div>

  [MORE SECTIONS...]
</div>

Copy the ENTIRE <div> from opening tag to closing </div> and paste it into Gemini.

FORMATTING NOTE:
The HTML may be long, but Gemini can handle it! The complete HTML ensures Gemini sees:
- All tables with all rows
- All expandable sections (audio explanations, common mistakes, examples)
- All formatting and emphasis
- Everything exactly as you see it on screen

EXAMPLE 2: TEXT-ONLY APPROACH (When you can't copy HTML)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Teach me French pronouns using this content:

PRONOUNS - KEY CONCEPTS:
- 6 subject pronouns: je (I), tu (you singular), il (he), elle (she), nous (we), vous (you plural/formal), ils (they masculine), elles (they feminine)
- Tu vs Vous: tu is informal/familiar, vous is formal/polite or plural
- Subject pronouns are used as the subject of the verb
- Example: Je parle français (I speak French)
- Example: Vous êtes intelligent (You are intelligent - formal)

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS:
- Tu = singular informal (friend, family, children)
- Vous = singular formal (boss, stranger, elderly) or plural (any group of 2+)

STUDY OBJECTIVES:
- Master all 6 subject pronouns
- Understand tu vs vous distinction
- Use pronouns correctly in sentences

EXAM REQUIREMENTS:
- SPEAKING: Say pronouns correctly and use them in sentences
- WRITING: Write sentences with correct pronoun usage
- READING: Identify pronouns in French text
- LISTENING: Hear pronouns and understand who they refer to

TEACH ME SLOWLY:
1. The 6 pronouns (je, tu, il/elle, nous, vous, ils/elles) — I'll check off when I can name all 6 and use them in sentences
2. Tu vs Vous distinction — I'll check off when I understand when to use each and can apply the rule
3. Pronouns in sentences — I'll check off after I can write 5 sentences with correct pronouns
